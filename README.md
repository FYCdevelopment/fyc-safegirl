# FYC SafeGirl - Hausa/Kanuri WhatsApp Chatbot for Girls Health & Safety
🌍 Empowering 50,000 Girls in Borno State, Nigeria | UNICEF FemTech 2026

## The Problem
In Borno State, 44.8% child marriage rate. 68% of adolescent girls lack confidential SRH information in Hausa/Kanuri. GBV survivors have no safe, anonymous reporting channel. Existing solutions are English-only and require internet.

## Our Solution
FYC SafeGirl is a low-data WhatsApp chatbot operating in Hausa, Kanuri & English:
- **1. Lafiyar Mata**: Menstrual health, hygiene, family planning myths
- **2. Rahoton GBV**: Anonymous GBV reporting linked to FYC case workers
- **3. Taimakon Zuciya**: Mental wellness, breathing exercises
- **4. Service Map**: Nearest clinic/safe space in Maiduguri, Jere, Konduga
- **USSD Fallback**: *347* for girls without smartphones

## Why FYC Development?
Foundation for Youth and Community Development - 5+ years in Borno IDP camps, trusted by 3,200+ girls, female case workers, community leaders support.

## Tech Stack (Open Source - MIT)
- WhatsApp Business API
- Hausa/Kanuri NLP dataset (to be open-sourced)
- GBV referral logic
